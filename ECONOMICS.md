# Economics

## What Is a Converted Lead Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Average 
startup buying credits spends ~$500-2000 on 
AI tools monthly. Credex offers 20-30% discount.

If a startup saves $400/month through Credex 
credits vs retail, and Credex margin is 10%:
- Customer spends $1,500/month on credits
- Credex margin: ~$150/month
- Annual LTV per customer: ~$1,800

Conservative estimate: **$500-1,800 LTV 
per converted customer.**

## Conversion Funnel

→ 300 complete audit (30% completion rate)
→ 60 submit email (20% of completers)
→ 12 book consultation (20% of email captures)
→ 4 buy credits (33% close rate)
