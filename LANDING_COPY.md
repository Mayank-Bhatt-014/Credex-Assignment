## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no trial period, no 
hidden fees. The audit is free forever. Credex 
makes money when users with large savings 
choose to buy discounted AI credits — not 
from charging you.

**Q: Will you share my data?**
A: Your tool names and savings numbers appear 
on your shareable URL. Your email and company 
name are never shown publicly. We do not sell 
your data to anyone.

**Q: How accurate is the audit?**
A: All pricing data is pulled from official 
vendor pricing pages and verified weekly. The 
recommendations are based on real plan 
comparisons — not estimates. A finance person 
should agree with every number.

**Q: Why is Credex offering this for free?**
A: SpendAudit surfaces real overspend. For 
users saving over $500/month, Credex discounted 
credits are often the next logical step. The 
tool is genuinely useful whether or not you 
ever talk to Credex.

**Q: What AI tools does it support?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, 
Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. 
More tools added based on user requests.
